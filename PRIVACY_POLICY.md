# Privacy Policy for Ludo Champion

**Last updated:** November 22, 2026

**Ludo Champion** ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of our players, especially children and families. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data.

---

### 1. Offline & No Personal Data Collection
- Ludo Champion is designed as an **offline board game**.
- We do **NOT** collect, request, store, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as names, phone numbers, email addresses, precise geolocation, device contacts, or photos.
- Custom player names and scores entered in the game are stored strictly locally on the user's device using local storage (`AsyncStorage`) and are never sent to external servers.

---

### 2. Google Play Families Policy & Children's Privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)
Our game is suitable for general audiences, children, teenagers, and families:
- **No Behavioral Profiling:** We do not track children across third-party websites or services.
- **No Gambling or Real Money:** Ludo Champion does not contain real-money betting, gambling mechanics, or loot boxes.
- **Child-Safe Content:** All artwork, sound effects, and gameplay elements are child-friendly and family-safe.

---

### 3. Advertising Policy (Future Integration)
When advertising is introduced in future releases:
- Only family-certified ad networks compliant with Google Play Families Policy will be utilized.
- All ad requests will explicitly enforce `tagForChildDirectedTreatment: true` and maximum ad rating `G` (General Audiences).
- Ads will **never** interrupt an active game match.

---

### 4. Device Permissions
Ludo Champion only requests permissions strictly required for gameplay:
- `VIBRATE`: Provides haptic feedback when rolling the dice, moving tokens, and capturing opponent tokens. Users can disable vibration anytime in the in-game Settings.
